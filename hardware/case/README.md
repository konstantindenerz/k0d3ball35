# Printed case

Parametric OpenSCAD model of the two-part case per half: a flat tray with angular walls that
follow the stepped PCB outline, a raised controller box with a separate screwed cap, a
battery well under the controller block, a pocket for the trackball cradle beside the right
PCB, and a hinged two-plate lid that covers keys and ball for transport (held by an inner lip)
and folds under the half as a tenting foot (four magnets in the floor and in the lid roof, a
2 mm steel pin in the hinge). Tickets: `issues/mech-case.md`, `issues/mech-case-ball-pocket.md`,
`issues/mech-case-lid.md`; design notes in `spec/11-case.md`.

Files

| `params.scad` | every dimension and every derived z value, documented |
| `data.scad` | layout facts for both halves, generated by `scripts/gen_case.py` (do not edit) |
| `logo_data.scad` | the wordmark as cell rectangles, generated by `scripts/gen_logo.py` (do not edit) |
| `common.scad` | 2D regions (PCB cavity, outer footprint, wells, pocket, box, PCB guard) |
| `logo.scad` | the owner wordmark scaled to millimetres and the groove that engraves it |
| `tray.scad` | tray, standoffs, bosses, controller box cap, pocket test part, dummies |
| `lid.scad` | hinged lid: outer and inner plate, inner lip, roof magnets, knuckle hinge with its stop, the folded foot |
| `Makefile` | `make` builds data, STL, DXF, previews and `stack.json` |
| `stack.py` | collects the echoed stack values into `stack.json` (left, right) |
| `retention.py` | does the clip keep the ball in and stay off it: writes `retention.json` (`make retention`) |
| `stl/` | printable parts per half: `lid-outer-*` and `lid-inner-*` are the two lid plates to print, `lid-*` the closed pair for the exploded view, `pocket-cover-right.stl` the flush cover over the cradle, `pocket-clip-right.stl` the optional retainer clip, `guard-*` the PCB guard between the switches, `pocket-test-right.stl` the test print of the pocket |
| `dxf/` | floor plan per half with every hole, for checking against the PCB drawings; `guard-*.dxf` the PCB guard outline for a laser-cut sheet |
| `preview/` | assembly, section through the ball, box close-up, lid on, lid folded as foot, hinge close-up, tray from below |

Requires OpenSCAD 2025 or newer with the Manifold backend (tested with 2026.10.11) and the
repository venv for the data generator. `make case` at the top level builds everything,
`make case-data` only refreshes `data.scad` after a layout change, `make -C hardware/case logo`
only `logo_data.scad` and `spec/images/logo.svg` after a change to `spec/images/logo.png`. `make case` rewrites the STLs;
the project page loads them from the repository, and `make site` refreshes the sizes and volumes
it lists - `tests/test_site.py` fails while those are stale.

## Geometry

Frame: the sources work in the layout frame, x outward towards the pinky, y towards the user,
origin at the middle-column top key, z = 0 is the PCB top (`hardware/layout/k0d3ball35.yaml`,
same as the cradle). With z up that frame is left-handed, while STL and DXF are always read
right-handed, so `case.scad` flips y once at export (`print_frame`). The files are in the print
frame: x outward, y away from the user, z up, which is how a slicer shows them from above with
the user at the front of the bed. The left half is also mirrored in x at the top level
(`mirror_left`), so both halves share every module. Numbers in `stack.json` and `data.scad` stay
in the layout frame. `tests/test_case_handedness.py` checks the STLs against the KiCad boards
(issues/mech-case-stl-mirrored.md: before 2026-11-10 every part was exported as its mirror image).

- Walls: 3.0 mm outside the PCB cavity (PCB plus 0.2 mm), mitre joins, no rounding. The rim
  is at +5.0, flush with the Choc keycap skirts. On the right half the edge runs in 90 degree
  steps around the ball pocket too (`case_outline`), and the pocket cover is flush with the rim.
- PCB guard (`guard`): 0.8 mm plate on the PCB top between the switches, 14.2 mm square cut-outs,
  3.7 mm holes over the screw heads, open over J1 (body and tails), the FFC path, the cradle
  and the inside of the box; it runs on under the box cap skirt, which stands on it. 1.2 mm
  under a pressed keycap, so no extra height, and no bare PCB visible from above. Outline from
  `gen_case.py` (`guard_polygon`), one piece, no web under 1.0 mm. Details in
  `spec/11-case.md` section 5a.
- Floor under the keys at -4.6 (3 mm under the PCB underside for hot-swap sockets and
  diodes); the underside is flat everywhere at -9.6.
- Five M2 standoffs (6.0 mm, 3.0 mm tall) under the PCB holes with heat-set inserts.
- Battery well under the controller block: bay plus the J3 and RB1 courtyards, each grown by
  1 mm, floor at -8.1, 6.5 mm under the PCB underside (5.7 mm cell, 0.4 mm solder joints, Kapton, 0.3 mm air, flush
  controller pins). The reset
  button is reached through a 3 mm pin hole in the floor, the slide switch lever through a
  7 x 3 mm slot in the outer wall directly under the PCB.
- Controller box: walls to +8.7 on the outer and USB side (the other two sides are inside
  the PCB and belong to the cap), USB-C opening 11 x 5.8 up to the cap plate, LED window
  3 x 3 in the inner wall 8 mm from the USB end, rebate for the cap plate, two corner bosses
  with inserts, 45 degree undersides.
- Cap (`box_cap`): 2 mm plate in the rebate plus an L-shaped 2 mm skirt (key side at
  x = -45.5 and thumb side at y = 50.6) that stands 0.3 mm above the PCB top; two M2 screws.
- Pocket (right half): part of the stepped edge and solid up to the rim; only `pocket_opening`
  (the well, the 28.4 mm cradle circle and the cable path to the board edge) stays open above
  the PCB plane, with a 4 mm ledge at +3.8 for the cover. Cover (`pocket_cover`): a 1.2 mm
  plate flush with the rim that reaches over the cup rim (0.375 mm above it) as a bezel round
  the ball: hole 20.55 mm (`cover_ball_gap` 0.75 to the ball, the cup is out of sight, the ball
  still lifts out), 0.4 mm chamfer on the top edge, two 2.4 mm holes for the clip's pins, two
  M2 x 6 countersunk into 1.6 mm pilots on the user-side ledge, ends at the PCB edge on the
  thumb side.
- Retainer clip (`pocket_clip`, optional, `stl/pocket-clip-right.stl`, centred on the ball like
  the cradle STL): a 1.2 mm C-ring on the cover round the hole with two claws over the ball's
  shoulders at +-80 degrees (0.4 mm off the ball), nothing in the 156 degree sector facing the
  thumb. Two split snap pins (2.2 mm, 0.9 mm slit, 0.12 mm undercut, prong strain 2.2 percent)
  go through the cover's pin holes at r 13, +-56 degrees, between the sensor tab (+x) and the claws.
  `retention.py` (`make retention`): trapped with 0.67 mm of combined looseness, 0.39 mm off the
  ball at rest, pulling it off never brings a claw closer, the pins keep 0.82 mm to the cradle.
  It keeps the shape of the earlier cup-mounted clip, which needed a 26.5 mm cover hole. At floor level the well
  is the 29 x 24 housing box (centred 2.55 mm off the ball towards +x) plus 0.5, which
  contains the cradle footprint (cup skirt plus tilted sleeve, 27.73 x 24.05 (x by y) with clearance),
  floor at -7.1 (2.5 mm floor), two 2.2 mm holes 20 mm apart on the y axis through the ball with a 4.8 mm, 1.3 mm deep
  90 degree countersink from below (`case_screw_cs_d`, `case_screw_cs_h`): the flat head of
  an M2 countersunk screw ends up 0.2 mm inside the underside, 1.2 mm of floor is left at
  the bore. The tray only keeps the lid lip off J1 and the cable corridor on the key side
  (`fpc_clear`).
- Foot magnets: four 6.2 mm pockets from below in the 5.0 mm floor under the key area next
  to the battery well. `gen_case.py` places them in the intersection of the outer lid plate's
  roof region (8 mm inside the outline, clear of the hinge strip) and the
  tray floor region shifted by `foot_dx`, clear of the wells, the FFC channel, J1 and the cable
  corridor, the standoffs and the feet: right (-36.94, 9.69), (-26.35, 17.69),
  (-26.35, 45.19), (-37.0, 38.94); left (-37.25, 9.69), (-29.478, 17.19), (-29.478, 45.19),
  (-37.19, 38.94). The outer lid plate's roof carries the matching pockets at the same points
  shifted by -`foot_dx` (lid x about 28 to 38, well inside the outer plate). The outer wall is
  smooth, no pads.
- Lid lip: a 1.0 mm lip on the inside of the lid skirt reaches 3.0 mm into the tray with
  `lid_fit` = 0.15 to the wall and holds the lid on top by friction; the lip stops at the
  controller box walls and the pocket cup. The keycaps end 1.6 mm inside the PCB edge, the
  lip 0.95 mm inside it, so 0.65 mm remain between lip and caps.
- Lid plates: the lid is split along the hinge line x = -13 (`hinge_x` in `data.scad`, the
  left half mirrored like everything else). The outer plate (pinky side) carries all four
  roof magnets; the inner plate carries the controller box notch (to +9.0) and the thumb
  cluster and is the kickstand of the foot. Roof underside +14.5 (`lid_clear` 3.5 over the
  caps), roof top +17.5 on both halves; the ball top (+13.95) stays under the roof, so there is
  no ball window. Within 6 mm of the line (`hinge_strip_w`) the roof is thickened downward to
  5 mm (`hinge_knuckle_d`), underside +12.5, 1.5 mm over the Choc caps.
- Hinge: 9 knuckles of 8.77 mm (`hinge_seg_len` target 10, odd count, 0.3 mm gaps) along y
  from -9.72 to +71.6 (span 81.3 mm); the outer plate owns the even knuckles including both
  ends. Axis at z = +15.0, knuckles flush with the roof top, radius 2.5; the groove in the
  other plate has radius 2.8 (`hinge_clear` 0.3) and the strip next to a groove is cleared
  so no thin lips remain. Pin: 2.0 mm steel rod (`hinge_pin_d`) in a 2.2 mm hole
  (`hinge_pin_fit` 0.2), about 80 mm long (span minus the 1.5 mm blind end, `hinge_blind`),
  inserted from the y-min end (away from the user); the y-max knuckle is blind so the pin
  cannot walk out. `-D hinge_pin_d=1.75` fits a piece of filament. Above the axis the two
  plates' vertical cut faces are 0.2 mm apart (`hinge_gap`).
- Stop: below the axis each plate's cut face is a plane through the axis leaning outward by
  `hinge_stop_deg`, half the solved fold (28.5 degrees right, 21.6 left), so closed there is a
  V slit at the skirt bottom (about 7.5 mm wide at the front and back skirt, 1.5 mm under the
  strip) and when folded by `hinge_fold_deg` (56.9 right, 43.2 left) the two faces meet flat
  on flat along the whole cut (skirt ends and strip underside) and carry the load. Knuckle and
  groove are round and never take the stop load. The fold is derived from `tent_deg` = 18 (the
  input): it is the angle at which the kickstand's lip edge reaches the desk.
- Feet: seven 10.5 mm recesses 0.8 mm deep for 1 mm rubber feet, which stand 0.2 mm proud
  (the five hole positions and two under the controller block).

## Printing

- Material: PETG or PLA, 0.2 mm layers (0.12 for the pocket test), 3 perimeters, 20 percent
  infill, no supports.
- Tray: flat on the bed, cavities open upwards. The only bridges are the USB opening (11 mm),
  the switch slot (7 mm), the LED window (3 mm) and the four foot magnet pockets from below
  (6 mm); the pocket opening is wider than its well, so it has no overhang.
  The boss undersides are 45 degree chamfers.
- Pocket cover: top down, no supports.
- Retainer clip: PETG, pins down on the bed, supports under the ring only (the claws rise from
  it on 45 degree backs). If it snaps in too hard or too loose, change `clip_pin_barb` by 0.03.
- Cap and both lid plates: roof down, no supports. The outer plate's roof magnet pockets open
  towards the roof underside, so they print as plain holes with the 0.6 mm skin on the bed;
  the lip's lead-in chamfer is a stepped taper at the top of the print. On the lid plates the
  horizontal 2.2 mm pin hole and the knuckle halves that protrude past the hinge line are the
  only small overhangs; the knuckle grooves in the skirt ends are the only upward faces.
- Wordmark: engraved 0.6 mm into the cap plate, which lies on the bed (the case underside can
  take it too with `logo_on_floor = true`, off by default). The grooves are one cell wide
  (1.0 mm on the cap, 0.9 mm on the floor), so every
  bridge is shorter than a millimetre and the first layer is the connected field between them.
  A filament change at 0.6 mm gives a flush two-colour wordmark. `logo_invert = true` engraves
  the field instead and leaves the lines proud, at the cost of a much thinner first layer.
- `pocket-test-right.stl` is the 50 x 50 mm corner of the right tray around the ball: print it
  with the cradle and a scrap PCB with the notch before the full tray.

## Inserts and screws

- PCB: M2 heat-set inserts (brass, M2 x 3 mm, outer diameter about 3.5) in the 3.2 mm holes
  of the five standoffs (4.8 mm deep) and the two cap bosses (4 mm deep). Press them with a
  soldering iron at about 200 C before the PCB goes in, flush with the top; 3.2 mm of floor
  stays under a standoff hole, the boss hole ends 0.4 mm above the chamfer and nothing can
  reach through. Screws: M2 x 5 machine screws from the top (M2 x 6 bottoms out in the cap
  bosses), head at most 3.5 mm (under the keycaps); the keycaps come off
  for assembly.
- Alternative: `-D pcb_screws="pilot"` turns the holes into 1.6 mm pilots for M2 self-tapping
  screws (6 mm deep).
- Cradle: two M2 x 8 countersunk self-tapping screws from below through the countersunk
  floor holes into the cradle's cup skirt (`hardware/trackball-cradle/README.md`).
- Pocket cover: two M2 x 6 countersunk screws from the top into 1.6 mm pilots in the ledge.

## Assembly

1. Press the seven inserts (five standoffs, two bosses).
2. Glue the magnets: the four tray floor pockets north pole down (towards the desk), the four
   pockets in the outer lid plate's roof south pole up, pushed in from the roof underside until
   they sit on the 0.6 mm skin. Check with a loose magnet that the lid snaps under the tray
   before the glue sets. The lid on top needs no magnets, the lip holds it.
3. Hinge: lay the two lid plates roof-up, align the knuckles and push the 2 mm rod in from
   the y-min end (back edge, away from the user) until it bottoms in the blind knuckle;
   optionally a drop of glue at the entry. The lid should fold to the stop without force.
4. Drop the PCB in (keycaps off), screw it to the standoffs, put the keycaps on.
5. Controller box: plug the controller, put the cap on (skirt towards the keys and the thumb
   keys), two M2 screws into the bosses.
6. Right half: stand the cradle module in the pocket, fasten the two M2 x 8 from below, route
   the FFC round the cup into J1. Pocket cover on (two M2 x 6 from the top), drop the ball in
   through it. Optional clip: claws left and right of the ball, press the pins into the cover
   until they click; pull it straight up to take it off.
7. Battery into the well, JST into J3, press the rubber feet into their recesses.
8. Lid on for transport (press it down until the lip is in). As a foot: fold the inner plate
   down to the stop, lay the outer plate roof-up against the tray underside under the
   controller half so the magnets snap it on, and stand the half on the desk on its outer
   bottom edge and the kickstand, which points inward towards the other half.

## Lid, hinge and foot

Closed, the two plates are one cover: skirt on the rim, lip in the tray, the knuckles flush
with the roof top, a 0.2 mm gap between the cut faces above the axis and the V slit of the
stop below it. Folded, the lid is the stand. The whole folded lid is shifted inward by
`foot_dx` (`data.scad`: -49.8 mm right, -67.8 left) so that the hinge axis sits 3 mm
(`foot_overhang`) outside the inner tray wall: the outer plate lies roof-up against the tray
underside under the controller half (its four roof magnets meet the four floor magnets,
invisible in both positions thanks to the 0.6 mm skin on the roof and the pockets from below
in the tray) and the inner plate folds down beyond the inner edge as a kickstand that points
inward, between the two halves, the two cut faces pressed flat on flat. The half stands on its
own outer bottom edge on the desk and on the hinge directly above the kickstand: nothing is
cantilevered, so a thumb press or a resting palm cannot pivot the board against the magnets,
which only keep the plate aligned. The four rubber feet at (-58, 1), (-58, 34), (-27, 8) and
(-32, 61) rest on the lid roof. Result (`stack.json`, right; left in brackets): `tent_deg` = 18
is the input, the fold `hinge_fold_deg` is solved so that the kickstand's lip edge reaches the
desk: 56.9 degrees (43.2), stop faces at `hinge_stop_deg` = half of that. The kickstand hangs
`foot_beta_deg` 38.9 degrees (25.2) below the tray, the hinge axis is 37.8 mm (37.8) above the
desk (`foot_hinge_h`), the kickstand's foot stands 34.5 mm (53.3) beyond the inner wall
(`foot_reach`) and the outer plate's free end floats 1.6 mm (7.2) above the desk
(`foot_free_end_h`; below about 13 degrees of tilt the lid thickness alone would hold the
board up, `tests/test_case.py` asserts more than 0.5 mm). `hinge_fold_override` (`params.scad`,
-1) shows another fold in `lid_folded` for the collision test only. The rubber feet stay
usable without the lid. Parts: `lid_outer` and `lid_inner` are the plates to print, `lid` the
closed pair, `lid_folded` the folded pair; the previews `lid_foot` (tilted on the desk) and
`lid_foot_flat` show the half on its lid.

## Open points

- LED window position (8 mm from the USB end, 3 x 3 in the inner wall) is a guess at the
  nice!nano LED; check against the controller before the first print.
- `box_inner_x` = -45.5 leaves 0.75 mm between the cap skirt and the inner column keycaps and
  1.5 mm to the controller; a wider box would hit the caps.
- `lid_fit` = 0.15 is a guess for a 0.4 mm nozzle; if the lid is loose or too tight on the
  first print, change it (0.1 tighter, 0.2 looser) and reprint only the lid plates.
- `hinge_clear` = 0.3 and `hinge_pin_fit` = 0.2 are guesses too: the knuckles must turn freely
  in the grooves and the rod must push in by hand. Check on the first print that the folded
  lid does not rock as a foot (the stop faces meet along the whole cut, the outer bottom edge
  and the kickstand's lip edge both touch the desk).
- As a foot the kickstand reaches about 54 mm (right; 57 left) inward beyond the inner wall,
  so the two halves need at least that gap between them; with both kickstands pointing at each
  other the halves cannot stand closer than about 11 cm.
- `cover_fit` = 0.15 in the rebate is a guess like `lid_fit`; the cover should drop in by hand
  and sit flush without rocking.
- The supplied wordmark is cropped at the top and `scripts/gen_logo.py` mirrors the frame foot
  in to close it; with the uncropped original that step can go.
