# Cover the FFC where it climbs beside the cup

## Context

Since r2.2 the sensor cable climbs the gap between the cradle cup and the PCB notch and folds
over the board edge into J1 on the key side. The gap is 2.95 mm wide, so about 5 mm of the 4.4 mm
wide cable is visible between the cup and the pocket wall, and the fold sits on the board edge.

Cosmetic only: the cable is static, the fold has a 1.5 mm radius, and the lid covers the whole
area in transport.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Small printed tab on the pocket wall (or a lip on the lid) that hides the cable without touching it
- [ ] Deburring note for the board edge under the fold in the build guide (FR4 router edges are sharp)

## Notes

Judge it on the first assembled half: it may look fine as it is.
